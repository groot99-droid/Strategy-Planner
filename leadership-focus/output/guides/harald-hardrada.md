# Harald Hardrada — Strategic Planning Guide

## Civilization VI Profile

**Civilization:** Norway  |  **Leader Title:** The Last Great Viking
**Source:** Base Game

**Leader Ability — Thunderbolt of the North**
Grants a large Production bonus toward naval melee units and enables coastal raiding (pillaging coastal tiles and cities directly from naval melee units), and unlocks the unique Viking Longship, supporting an aggressive naval-raiding playstyle. (Harald also has an alternate 'Varangian' persona, added later, with a different ability, Varangian Guard, focused on cheaply levying and maintaining city-state units.)

**Agenda — Last Viking King**
Harald wants to build a powerful navy, respects other civilizations with strong naval forces, and looks down on those with weak ones.

**Civilization Ability — Knarr**
Norwegian units can cross Ocean tiles once Shipbuilding is researched, suffer no extra Movement cost for embarking or disembarking, and naval melee units can heal even in neutral (unowned) territory, giving Norway unmatched early naval mobility.

**Unique Units**
- Berserker (replaces Man-At-Arms) — A strong early melee unit that gains a further boost once Military Tactics is researched, fitting Norway's aggressive coastal-raiding identity.
- Viking Longship (replaces Galley) — A cheaper, faster, and stronger early naval unit than the Galley it replaces, able to heal in neutral waters thanks to the Knarr civ ability, ideal for early raiding.

**Unique Infrastructure**
- Stave Church (Building) — Provides Faith and additional benefits, and in the Varangian persona also grants Influence points, supporting Norway's religious and diplomatic play alongside its raiding.

### Historical Background
Harald Hardrada ('Hard Ruler') was King of Norway from 1046 to 1066 and is often called the last great king of the Viking Age. Before taking the Norwegian throne he spent years as a mercenary commander in the Byzantine Empire's elite Varangian Guard, the historical basis for his alternate in-game persona. He died in 1066 at the Battle of Stamford Bridge in England while attempting to conquer the English throne, a defeat just weeks before the Norman Conquest that many historians treat as marking the symbolic end of the Viking Age — a fittingly aggressive, sea-borne legacy reflected in Norway's naval-raiding leader ability and civ-wide ocean mobility.

## Leadership Focus Guide

**Shape:** Monitor
**Curve:** Ramp | **Conversion:** Conditional | **Condition cost:** Coastal-heavy map dependency

> The core loop is a stat check, not a build order. These guides fail hardest when written as sequences, because the player's real job is watching one number and responding to it.

### Setup
*Turns: 1 to roughly 25*

Establish the thing being monitored and the infrastructure that lets you respond to it.

- Name the watched stat on turn 1: Un-pillaged coastal tile inventory near current fleet position
- Establish its bands before it matters, so the player recognizes trouble the first time rather than the third.
- Build the response capacity. A monitor with no available response is just anxiety.

### Standing Loop
*Turns: 25 to end of game*

This replaces the turn list entirely. The guide is this loop plus the era checkpoints.

- Read Un-pillaged coastal tile inventory near current fleet position.
- Green band: continue current build. No action.
- Amber band: begin the correction now. Amber is the acting band; red is the failing band.
- Red band: the ability is off or about to be. Everything else is deprioritized until it clears.

### Era Checkpoints
*Turns: each era boundary*

Re-read the role. Monitor leaders change what the stat means across eras.

- Ancient/Classical: Longship raiding as primary income.
- Medieval/Renaissance: Still raiding, with diminishing returns per coast.
- Industrial/Modern: The pillage economy is obsolete; coastal defenses outclass raiders.
- Atomic/Information: Nothing.

### Abort branch
**Trigger:** Watched stat sits in the red band for more than roughly 15 turns with no recovery path.
- Stop paying the Coastal-heavy map dependency. If the bonus is unreachable, its price is pure loss.
- Rebuild as a generic civ around whatever the monitored strategy already produced.

### Fail state
The stat crossed into red and stayed. For Harald Hardrada, Pillage yields deplete per tile. The same coastline does not pay twice.

### Caveats
- Pillage yields deplete per tile. The same coastline does not pay twice.
- Raiding produces income but never territory, so the empire does not grow while the ability works.
- Landlocked or low-coast maps flatly disable the strategy.

### Guide must account for
- A parallel domestic build plan, since raiding alone never compounds.
- A trigger for when to stop raiding and start actually taking cities.
- A map viability check before the strategy is recommended at all.
