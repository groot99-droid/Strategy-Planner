# Matthias Corvinus — Strategic Planning Guide

## Civilization VI Profile

**Civilization:** Hungary  |  **Leader Title:** The Raven King
**Source:** Gathering Storm

**Leader Ability — Raven King**
Units levied from city-states gain bonus Movement and Combat Strength, cost far less Gold and resources to upgrade, and levying troops from a city-state grants 2 extra Envoys there, making city-state levying a core Hungarian military strategy.

**Agenda — Raven Banner**
Matthias Corvinus frequently levies units from city-states and admires other leaders who do the same, while disliking those who avoid using mercenaries.

**Civilization Ability — Pearl of the Danube**
Districts and buildings built across a River from the City Center receive a large Production bonus, rewarding cities founded and developed along rivers.

**Unique Units**
- Black Army (replaces Man-At-Arms) — An early professional-army unit that upgrades directly into Hungary's second unique unit, giving Hungary a long-lasting line of powerful cavalry-adjacent forces.
- Huszár (replaces none (upgrade of Black Army)) — A strong light cavalry unit that continues Hungary's unique military line into the medieval era, well suited to levied-army compositions.

**Unique Infrastructure**
- Thermal Bath (Building) — Provides Amenities and helps prevent revolt/loyalty problems in newly conquered or levy-heavy cities, supporting Hungary's mercenary-driven playstyle.

### Historical Background
Matthias Corvinus ruled the Kingdom of Hungary from 1458 to 1490, nicknamed 'the Raven King' for his family's raven emblem (Corvinus), and is remembered as one of Renaissance Europe's most capable rulers. He built the Black Army of Hungary, one of Europe's first large professional standing armies, financed partly through mercenaries and levied forces, directly reflected in his in-game ability and agenda around cheaply levying city-state units. He also turned his court at Buda into a major center of Renaissance humanism, attracting scholars and artists from across Europe, and his Hungary sat astride the Danube, echoed by the civilization's river-adjacency production bonus.

## Leadership Focus Guide

**Shape:** Monitor
**Curve:** Ramp | **Conversion:** Conditional | **Condition cost:** Ongoing gold and envoy spend; levied units are rented, not owned

> The core loop is a stat check, not a build order. These guides fail hardest when written as sequences, because the player's real job is watching one number and responding to it.

### Setup
*Turns: 1 to roughly 25*

Establish the thing being monitored and the infrastructure that lets you respond to it.

- Name the watched stat on turn 1: Gold reserve vs. rising levy cost, and suzerainty status of levied city-states
- Establish its bands before it matters, so the player recognizes trouble the first time rather than the third.
- Build the response capacity. A monitor with no available response is just anxiety.

### Standing Loop
*Turns: 25 to end of game*

This replaces the turn list entirely. The guide is this loop plus the era checkpoints.

- Read Gold reserve vs. rising levy cost, and suzerainty status of levied city-states.
- Green band: continue current build. No action.
- Amber band: begin the correction now. Amber is the acting band; red is the failing band.
- Red band: the ability is off or about to be. Everything else is deprioritized until it clears.

### Era Checkpoints
*Turns: each era boundary*

Re-read the role. Monitor leaders change what the stat means across eras.

- Ancient/Classical: Envoy and gold economy. Not yet a military civ.
- Medieval/Renaissance: Peak. Levied proxy armies at full effect.
- Industrial/Modern: Levied city-state units lag badly behind modern militaries.
- Atomic/Information: Nothing.

### Abort branch
**Trigger:** Watched stat sits in the red band for more than roughly 15 turns with no recovery path.
- Stop paying the Ongoing gold and envoy spend; levied units are rented, not owned. If the bonus is unreachable, its price is pure loss.
- Rebuild as a generic civ around whatever the monitored strategy already produced.

### Fail state
The stat crossed into red and stayed. For Matthias Corvinus, Levy costs scale, so the strategy gets more expensive exactly as you use it more.

### Caveats
- Levy costs scale, so the strategy gets more expensive exactly as you use it more.
- Suzerainty can be taken mid-campaign, which removes the army you are currently fighting with.
- Levied units revert. Nothing you levy is ever yours.

### Guide must account for
- A gold reserve threshold checked before each levy, not after.
- Suzerainty defense as an ongoing per-turn concern, tracked like a resource.
- Captured city consolidation, since the cities are the only permanent gain.
