# Kristina — Strategic Planning Guide

## Civilization VI Profile

**Civilization:** Sweden  |  **Leader Title:** Queen of Sweden
**Source:** Gathering Storm

**Leader Ability — Minerva of the North**
Any building with at least 3 Great Work slots and any Wonder with at least 2 Great Work slots is automatically 'themed' once all its slots are filled (no need to manually match eras/types), and Kristina can build the unique Queen's Bibliothèque in the Government Plaza — a 6-slot Great Work building (2 each for Writing, Art, and Music) that also grants Great Person points, a Governor Title, and a policy card, making Sweden extremely efficient at Culture Victory play.

**Agenda — Bibliophile**
Kristina tries to collect as many Great Works as possible and likes leaders who leave Great Works for her to acquire; she dislikes rivals who compete hard for the same Great Works.

**Civilization Ability — Nobel Prize**
Sweden gains bonus Great Scientist and Great Engineer points from Universities and Factories, generates Diplomatic Favor for every Great Person it recruits, and unlocks three unique World Congress competitions from the Industrial era onward, blending science, culture, and diplomacy.

**Unique Units**
- Carolean (replaces Pike and Shot) — A Renaissance era unit with extra Movement and a Combat Strength bonus for each point of unused Movement, plus bonus strength against cavalry, rewarding aggressive, mobile play.

**Unique Infrastructure**
- Open-Air Museum (Improvement) — Grants Culture and Tourism scaling with the variety of terrain types (Snow, Tundra, Desert, Plains, Grassland) Swedish cities are founded on, up to one per city, rewarding geographically diverse settling.

### Historical Background
Kristina (1626-1689) was Queen of Sweden from 1632 to 1654, a famously erudite and unconventional monarch who turned Stockholm into a hub for scholars, philosophers (including René Descartes, who died there), and artists, earning Sweden the nickname 'Athens of the North' during her reign — the direct inspiration for her in-game 'Minerva of the North' ability and Great Works focus. She abdicated the throne in 1654, converted to Catholicism (a controversial act for a Lutheran monarch), and spent much of her later life in Rome as a patron of the arts and sciences. Her personal passion for collecting books, art, and manuscripts is reflected in Sweden's Great Works/Bibliophile-centered kit.

## Leadership Focus Guide

**Shape:** Dead-Phase
**Curve:** Bloom | **Conversion:** Permanent | **Condition cost:** None mechanically, but no early-game identity at all

> The hardest template to write and the most necessary. The leader contributes nothing for 80 to 120 turns. Without an explicit early game AND an explicit statement that the flatness is intended, players abandon the plan around turn 60.

### Phase A — Dormant
*Turns: 1 to roughly 140*

Survive, develop, and quietly accumulate the prerequisite the payoff phase consumes.

- Run a complete generic build order. The guide must supply this in full, because the leader supplies no direction.
- Maintain a defensive floor. Elimination during Phase A is the single most common way these leaders lose, and it is invisible in the ability description.
- Accumulate the prerequisite even though it produces nothing yet: Accumulated Great Person points and a stock of undisplayed Great Works
- Provide non-ability progress markers so the player has feedback: city count, science rate, district count. Something must be visibly improving.

### Phase B — Activation
*Turns: roughly 140*

The kit switches on. Verify the accumulated prerequisite is actually sufficient.

- Check the stockpile. Activation with an empty stockpile wastes the entire dormant phase.
- Peak. Automatic theming across the empire.
- Reorient production toward the payoff. Phase A build habits are now wrong.

### Phase C — Payoff
*Turns: 140 onward*

Convert the accumulated position at speed. The remaining game is short, so this phase runs hot.

- Effortless tourism output.
- Push the victory condition directly. There is no time for a second build-out.

### Abort branch
**Trigger:** Reaching activation with the prerequisite stockpile empty, or having lost cities during the dormant phase.
- Accept a lesser victory path built on the generic empire rather than the leader ability.
- The leader ability is now decorative. Say so and stop planning around it.

### Fail state
Crippled or eliminated before activation, or arriving at activation with nothing stockpiled. Both are Phase A failures that only become visible in Phase B.

### Caveats
- Roughly 80 turns with no leader-specific direction at all.
- Auto-theming needs Great Works, which need Great People, which need early investment with no visible return.
- The Queen's Library is a one-time effect, not an engine.

### Guide must account for
- A full survival and development build order for the dormant phase.
- Great Person point accumulation started early despite producing nothing immediately.
- An explicit note that the flat early curve is intended, so the plan is not abandoned.
