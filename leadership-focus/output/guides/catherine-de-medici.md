# Catherine de Medici (Black Queen) — Strategic Planning Guide

## Civilization VI Profile

**Civilization:** France  |  **Leader Title:** Queen of France
**Source:** Base Game (Black Queen is Catherine's original/default persona; the alternate 'Magnificence' persona was added later via New Frontier Pass Persona Packs)

**Leader Ability — Catherine's Flying Squadron**
As the Black Queen persona (her default, original ability), Catherine gains one extra level of Diplomatic Visibility with every civilization she has met, receives a free Spy upon researching Castles, and all her Spies start with a free promotion — making her the strongest espionage leader in the game. (Her alternate Magnificence persona instead focuses on collecting and trading Luxury resources via the agenda 'Sumptuous Finery.')

**Agenda — Black Queen**
Catherine wants to accumulate as many Spies and as much Diplomatic Visibility as possible, and dislikes leaders who ignore espionage entirely.

**Civilization Ability — Grand Tour**
France gets bonus Production toward Medieval, Renaissance, and Industrial era Wonders, and doubles the Tourism generated by Wonders of any era, making France one of the strongest wonder-rush/culture-victory civilizations.

**Unique Units**
- Garde Impériale (replaces Line Infantry (later replaces the Musketman-tier military unit chain)) — An Industrial era melee unit with higher base Combat Strength and a large bonus fighting on the capital's home continent, plus Great General points from kills.

**Unique Infrastructure**
- Château (Improvement) — Must be built adjacent to a river and provides Culture, with bonus Culture the closer it is to a Wonder, reinforcing France's wonder-and-culture strategy.

### Historical Background
Catherine de' Medici (1519-1589) was an Italian-born queen consort and later regent of France during the tumultuous French Wars of Religion, wielding influence over her sons' reigns as king. She earned a fearsome reputation — fairly or not — for court intrigue, poison plots, and involvement in events like the St. Bartholomew's Day Massacre, cementing the 'Black Queen' legend that names her default in-game persona and its espionage/spy focus. Her family's Medici banking and political dynasty in Florence also connects to her later New Frontier persona, 'Magnificence,' which instead emphasizes courtly luxury and the collection of fine resources, echoing the opulent Medici court culture.

## Leadership Focus Guide

**Shape:** Linear
**Curve:** Flat | **Conversion:** Permanent | **Condition cost:** None

> Standard era-block guides work. The ability amplifies a plan rather than supplying one, so the guide's real job is making the player choose a direction the amplifier can act on.

### Ancient / Classical
*Turns: 1 to 80*
- Free spy and early diplomatic visibility.
- Choose the victory path now. A permanent, unconditional bonus applied to no particular plan produces a comfortable game and no win.
- Respect the one constraint: None

### Medieval / Renaissance
*Turns: 80 to 160*
- Espionage economy plus combat visibility bonuses.
- Era checkpoint: confirm the bonus is being spent, not banked. This is the characteristic failure of the shape.

### Industrial / Modern
*Turns: 160 to 240*
- Chateau tourism coming online.
- Era checkpoint: is the chosen victory path still reachable? Linear leaders can pivot; the flat curve makes it affordable.

### Atomic / Information
*Turns: 240 onward*
- Tourism and intelligence together.
- Close out the chosen condition.

### Abort branch
**Trigger:** Turn 150 with no victory path committed.
- Pick the path the accumulated empire is already closest to and commit fully. Linear leaders lose by drifting, not by collapsing.

### Fail state
A comfortable, undirected empire that never converts its advantage into a win condition.

### Caveats
- Spies get caught and killed. The free spy is not a permanent asset.
- The visibility combat bonus only matters if you actually go to war.
- Chateaux require rivers, so the late payoff depends on early land decisions.

### Guide must account for
- Spy mission priority that shifts by era rather than a fixed assignment.
- River tiles reserved for Chateaux from the early game onward.
- A decision on whether war is part of the plan, since it changes the value of visibility.
