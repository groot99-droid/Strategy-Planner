# Mansa Musa — Leadership Focus Guide

**Shape:** Monitor
**Curve:** Flat | **Conversion:** Permanent | **Condition cost:** Severe Production penalty and desert dependency

> The core loop is a stat check, not a build order. These guides fail hardest when written as sequences, because the player's real job is watching one number and responding to it.

## Setup
*Turns: 1 to roughly 25*

Establish the thing being monitored and the infrastructure that lets you respond to it.

- Name the watched stat on turn 1: Gold income per turn against upcoming purchase costs
- Establish its bands before it matters, so the player recognizes trouble the first time rather than the third.
- Build the response capacity. A monitor with no available response is just anxiety.

## Standing Loop
*Turns: 25 to end of game*

This replaces the turn list entirely. The guide is this loop plus the era checkpoints.

- Read Gold income per turn against upcoming purchase costs.
- Green band: continue current build. No action.
- Amber band: begin the correction now. Amber is the acting band; red is the failing band.
- Red band: the ability is off or about to be. Everything else is deprioritized until it clears.

## Era Checkpoints
*Turns: each era boundary*

Re-read the role. Monitor leaders change what the stat means across eras.

- Ancient/Classical: Desert settling with weak production and growing Gold.
- Medieval/Renaissance: Purchasing infrastructure faster than rivals can build it.
- Industrial/Modern: Buying whatever the game requires.
- Atomic/Information: Strong if the Gold engine held; helpless if it did not.

## Abort branch
**Trigger:** Watched stat sits in the red band for more than roughly 15 turns with no recovery path.
- Stop paying the Severe Production penalty and desert dependency. If the bonus is unreachable, its price is pure loss.
- Rebuild as a generic civ around whatever the monitored strategy already produced.

## Fail state
The stat crossed into red and stayed. For Mansa Musa, The production penalty makes Wonders effectively unavailable for the whole game.

## Caveats
- The production penalty makes Wonders effectively unavailable for the whole game.
- Desert food scarcity limits population, which limits the Gold engine itself.
- An economic collapse is unrecoverable, because there is no production fallback.

## Guide must account for
- A Gold income floor defined per era, treated as a survival threshold.
- An explicit never-build-always-buy rule, since the instinct to build wastes turns.
- Wonders removed from the plan entirely rather than listed as optional.
