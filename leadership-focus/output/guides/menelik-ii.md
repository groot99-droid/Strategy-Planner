# Menelik II — Strategic Planning Guide

## Civilization VI Profile

**Civilization:** Ethiopia  |  **Leader Title:** none
**Source:** New Frontier Pass

**Leader Ability — Council of Ministers**
Ethiopian cities founded on Hills gain Science and Culture equal to a share of the Faith they generate, and Ethiopian units get a combat strength bonus when fighting on Hills, rewarding a hill-focused settling pattern.

**Agenda — Ethiopian Highlands**
Menelik prefers to settle on Hills himself and likes civilizations that leave hill-heavy regions for Ethiopia to claim; he dislikes rivals who also aggressively settle Hills.

**Civilization Ability — Aksumite Legacy**
Ethiopian cities gain bonus Faith from resources, amplified further when international Trade Routes originate from resource-rich cities, and Ethiopia can purchase Archaeology Museums and Archaeologists with Faith.

**Unique Units**
- Oromo Cavalry (replaces Courser) — A light cavalry unit with improved Combat Strength and Sight that suffers no movement penalty on Hills, fitting Ethiopia's hill-based playstyle.

**Unique Infrastructure**
- Rock-Hewn Church (Improvement) — A unique tile improvement buildable only on Hills that generates strong Faith and cannot be destroyed by natural disasters, reinforcing Ethiopia's Faith-centric hill economy.

### Historical Background
Menelik II (reigned 1889-1913) was Emperor of Ethiopia who modernized the country's military and infrastructure while decisively defeating an invading Italian army at the Battle of Adwa in 1896, preserving Ethiopian sovereignty during the era of European colonization of Africa. His reign built on centuries of Ethiopian Christian tradition, including the rock-hewn churches carved into hillsides at places like Lalibela centuries earlier, and expanded the empire's territory significantly. The in-game emphasis on Hills, Faith, and rock-hewn architecture directly reflects Ethiopia's highland geography and its long Christian religious heritage.

## Leadership Focus Guide

**Shape:** Linear
**Curve:** Flat | **Conversion:** Permanent | **Condition cost:** Hill settling requirement

> Standard era-block guides work. The ability amplifies a plan rather than supplying one, so the guide's real job is making the player choose a direction the amplifier can act on.

### Ancient / Classical
*Turns: 1 to 80*
- Hill settling and early Faith generation.
- Choose the victory path now. A permanent, unconditional bonus applied to no particular plan produces a comfortable game and no win.
- Respect the one constraint: Hill settling requirement

### Medieval / Renaissance
*Turns: 80 to 160*
- Faith converting into Science and Culture simultaneously.
- Era checkpoint: confirm the bonus is being spent, not banked. This is the characteristic failure of the shape.

### Industrial / Modern
*Turns: 160 to 240*
- One yield stream funding three victory paths.
- Era checkpoint: is the chosen victory path still reachable? Linear leaders can pivot; the flat curve makes it affordable.

### Atomic / Information
*Turns: 240 onward*
- Flexible and self-sufficient.
- Close out the chosen condition.

### Abort branch
**Trigger:** Turn 150 with no victory path committed.
- Pick the path the accumulated empire is already closest to and commit fully. Linear leaders lose by drifting, not by collapsing.

### Fail state
A comfortable, undirected empire that never converts its advantage into a win condition.

### Caveats
- Hills mean low food, which caps the population that drives everything else.
- The conversion is a percentage, so a weak Faith base converts into very little.
- Rock-Hewn Church placement has terrain requirements that constrain city layout.

### Guide must account for
- Faith base construction prioritized before the conversion is worth anything.
- A food supplement plan for hill-heavy cities.
- A victory path decision, since the kit funds three without preferring one.
