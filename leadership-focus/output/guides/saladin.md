# Saladin — Strategic Planning Guide

## Civilization VI Profile

**Civilization:** Arabia  |  **Leader Title:** Vizier
**Source:** Base Game

**Leader Ability — Righteousness of the Faith**
The worship building of Saladin's religion can be purchased by any civilization at a steep Faith discount, and it grants extra Science, Faith, and Culture to the cities that have it, letting Arabia's religion spread its economic benefits far beyond its own borders.

**Agenda — Ayyubid Dynasty**
Saladin favors leaders who have adopted his religion's worship building and likes those who avoid war with its followers; he dislikes leaders who follow a different religion or attack civilizations that share his faith.

**Civilization Ability — The Last Prophet**
Arabia is guaranteed to receive the final available Great Prophet, virtually ensuring it can always found a religion, and it gains bonus Science for every foreign city following Arabia's religion.

**Unique Units**
- Mamluk (replaces Knight) — A heavy cavalry unit that heals every turn regardless of location, giving Arabian armies unusual staying power on extended campaigns.

**Unique Infrastructure**
- Madrasa (Building) — Replaces the University with higher base Science and a Faith bonus equal to the city's Campus adjacency bonus, letting Arabia convert religious strength into science output.

### Historical Background
Saladin (Salah ad-Din, c. 1137-1193) was the Kurdish founder of the Ayyubid dynasty who unified Egypt and Syria under his rule and became the preeminent Muslim leader of the Crusades. He is best remembered for recapturing Jerusalem from the Crusader states in 1187 and for his chivalrous conduct toward defeated enemies, including Richard the Lionheart, which earned him a reputation for both military skill and honor across cultures. His deep association with founding and defending a shared faith across a wide territory underlies Arabia's in-game identity as the civilization most reliably able to found and spread a religion.

## Leadership Focus Guide

**Shape:** Linear
**Curve:** Ramp | **Conversion:** Permanent | **Condition cost:** None

> Standard era-block guides work. The ability amplifies a plan rather than supplying one, so the guide's real job is making the player choose a direction the amplifier can act on.

### Ancient / Classical
*Turns: 1 to 80*
- Skip the Faith race entirely. Campuses and expansion.
- Choose the victory path now. A permanent, unconditional bonus applied to no particular plan produces a comfortable game and no win.
- Respect the one constraint: None

### Medieval / Renaissance
*Turns: 80 to 160*
- Religion arrives; Madrasa science comes online.
- Era checkpoint: confirm the bonus is being spent, not banked. This is the characteristic failure of the shape.

### Industrial / Modern
*Turns: 160 to 240*
- Strong combined science and faith.
- Era checkpoint: is the chosen victory path still reachable? Linear leaders can pivot; the flat curve makes it affordable.

### Atomic / Information
*Turns: 240 onward*
- Solid science position.
- Close out the chosen condition.

### Abort branch
**Trigger:** Turn 150 with no victory path committed.
- Pick the path the accumulated empire is already closest to and commit fully. Linear leaders lose by drifting, not by collapsing.

### Fail state
A comfortable, undirected empire that never converts its advantage into a win condition.

### Caveats
- The guaranteed prophet is the last one, so belief selection is whatever others left behind.
- A late religion means rival religions may already have spread into your cities.
- The free tempo only pays if it is actually spent on science and expansion, not banked.

### Guide must account for
- A belief plan that assumes leftovers rather than first choice.
- Religious defense against earlier-founded faiths already pressuring your cities.
- An explicit use for the skipped early Faith investment, or the advantage disappears.
