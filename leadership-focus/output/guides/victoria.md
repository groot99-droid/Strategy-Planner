# Victoria (Age of Steam) — Strategic Planning Guide

## Civilization VI Profile

**Civilization:** English  |  **Leader Title:** Queen of the United Kingdom (Age of Steam persona)
**Source:** New Frontier Pass (Leader Pass persona)

**Leader Ability — Age of Steam**
Cities gain +10% Production for every Industrial Zone building present in them, and all Strategic Resources in England's territory (even unimproved ones) receive a flat Production bonus, making this Victoria persona a heavy Industrial-era production and Strategic Resource specialist rather than the original 'Pax Britannica' overseas-expansion leader.

**Agenda — Industrial Revolution**
This Victoria persona pushes hard to build up industry and favors civilizations with high Production output, while looking down on those with low Production.

**Civilization Ability — Workshop of the World**
England gets bonus yields from Iron and Coal resources, greatly boosted Military Engineer production and extra Military Engineer charges, bonus yields from powered buildings, extra Production toward Industrial Zones, and increased strategic resource stockpiles from Harbors -- an Industrial-Revolution-themed civilization ability shared by all English leaders.

**Unique Units**
- Sea Dog (replaces Privateer) — A unique naval raider that upgrades into the Submarine line and is strong at harassing enemy trade routes and coastal territory during the early-modern naval era; note the Redcoat unique unit is tied specifically to the alternate Victoria (Age of Empire) persona's leader ability, not to Age of Steam.

**Unique Infrastructure**
- Royal Navy Dockyard (District) — Replaces the Harbor; grants a free naval unit when first built and extra Trade Route capacity/unit movement, reinforcing England's naval and trade-route strengths.

### Historical Background
Queen Victoria reigned over the United Kingdom from 1837 to 1901, a period that saw Britain become the dominant global industrial and colonial power, with an empire famously described as one 'on which the sun never sets.' Her reign coincided with the height of the Industrial Revolution -- steam-powered railways, factories, and the Royal Navy's coal-fired fleet all expanded dramatically during this era, which is exactly what this 'Age of Steam' persona's Industrial Zone and Strategic Resource bonuses represent. This persona (added via the Leader Pass's Rulers of England content) is a deliberately narrower, industrially-focused counterpart to the original 'Age of Empire' Victoria persona, whose ability and Redcoat unit instead modeled 19th-century British colonial expansion overseas.

## Leadership Focus Guide

**Shape:** Dead-Phase
**Curve:** Bloom | **Conversion:** Permanent | **Condition cost:** Iron and coal access; percentage bonuses need an existing base to multiply

> The hardest template to write and the most necessary. The leader contributes nothing for 80 to 120 turns. Without an explicit early game AND an explicit statement that the flatness is intended, players abandon the plan around turn 60.

### Phase A — Dormant
*Turns: 1 to roughly 120*

Survive, develop, and quietly accumulate the prerequisite the payoff phase consumes.

- Run a complete generic build order. The guide must supply this in full, because the leader supplies no direction.
- Maintain a defensive floor. Elimination during Phase A is the single most common way these leaders lose, and it is invisible in the ability description.
- Accumulate the prerequisite even though it produces nothing yet: Iron and coal secured, and a functioning Industrial Zone in most cities
- Provide non-ability progress markers so the player has feedback: city count, science rate, district count. Something must be visibly improving.

### Phase B — Activation
*Turns: roughly 120*

The kit switches on. Verify the accumulated prerequisite is actually sufficient.

- Check the stockpile. Activation with an empty stockpile wastes the entire dormant phase.
- Peak. Stacking percentage production across the empire.
- Reorient production toward the payoff. Phase A build habits are now wrong.

### Phase C — Payoff
*Turns: 120 onward*

Convert the accumulated position at speed. The remaining game is short, so this phase runs hot.

- Dominant production for space race or military output.
- Push the victory condition directly. There is no time for a second build-out.

### Abort branch
**Trigger:** Reaching activation with the prerequisite stockpile empty, or having lost cities during the dormant phase.
- Accept a lesser victory path built on the generic empire rather than the leader ability.
- The leader ability is now decorative. Say so and stop planning around it.

### Fail state
Crippled or eliminated before activation, or arriving at activation with nothing stockpiled. Both are Phase A failures that only become visible in Phase B.

### Caveats
- Percentage bonuses on a small base produce almost nothing. Empire size before Industrial decides the payoff.
- Coal and iron access is a hard prerequisite the ability assumes but does not provide.
- Roughly 120 turns with no leader-specific direction at all.

### Guide must account for
- A full generic early and mid game build order, because the kit supplies none.
- A strategic resource securing deadline set before the Industrial era arrives.
- An explicit statement that the flat early curve is expected, so players do not abandon the plan.
