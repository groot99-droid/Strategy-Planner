# Simon Bolivar — Strategic Planning Guide

## Civilization VI Profile

**Civilization:** Gran Colombia  |  **Leader Title:** El Libertador
**Source:** New Frontier Pass (Leader Pass persona)

**Leader Ability — Campaña Admirable**
Whenever the game advances to a new era, Bolívar gains a Comandante General, a unique Great Person-style military leader whose presence heals and strengthens nearby Gran Colombian units, reinforcing sustained campaigns of conquest.

**Agenda — Carabobo**
Bolívar respects leaders who build up large numbers of promoted, veteran military units, and favors civilizations that invest heavily in Encampments and unit experience.

**Civilization Ability — Ejército Patriota**
Gran Colombian units gain extra Movement and can be promoted without ending their turn, allowing units to level up and reposition in the same turn for highly mobile offensives.

**Unique Units**
- Llanero (replaces Cavalry) — Costs less to maintain, gains a combat bonus for each adjacent Llanero, and fully heals near an active Comandante General, rewarding massed cavalry pushes.

**Unique Infrastructure**
- Hacienda (Improvement) — A unique tile improvement unlocked with the Mercantilism civic that provides Production, Gold, and Housing, with extra Food for each adjacent Plantation, strengthening the economic base behind Gran Colombia's mobile armies.

### Historical Background
Simón Bolívar was the preeminent leader of the early 19th-century Spanish American wars of independence, liberating what are now Venezuela, Colombia, Ecuador, Panama, Peru, and Bolivia from Spanish colonial rule. Revered across Latin America as 'El Libertador,' he envisioned a unified South American state, Gran Colombia, which he helped found in 1819 but which fractured into separate nations shortly after his death in 1830. His campaigns depended on rapid, long-distance cavalry movement across difficult terrain, reflected in-game by his civilization's extra-movement and rapid-promotion abilities and the mobile Llanero cavalry unit named for the horsemen of the Venezuelan plains who formed the backbone of his armies.

## Leadership Focus Guide

**Shape:** Linear
**Curve:** Flat | **Conversion:** Permanent | **Condition cost:** None

> Standard era-block guides work. The ability amplifies a plan rather than supplying one, so the guide's real job is making the player choose a direction the amplifier can act on.

### Ancient / Classical
*Turns: 1 to 80*
- Accelerated settling. Settlers and Builders move faster than anyone's.
- Choose the victory path now. A permanent, unconditional bonus applied to no particular plan produces a comfortable game and no win.
- Respect the one constraint: None

### Medieval / Renaissance
*Turns: 80 to 160*
- Fast wars with stacked Comandante Generals.
- Era checkpoint: confirm the bonus is being spent, not banked. This is the characteristic failure of the shape.

### Industrial / Modern
*Turns: 160 to 240*
- Still faster than everyone. The bonus never stops applying.
- Era checkpoint: is the chosen victory path still reachable? Linear leaders can pivot; the flat curve makes it affordable.

### Atomic / Information
*Turns: 240 onward*
- Still relevant. One of the few kits with no expiry.
- Close out the chosen condition.

### Abort branch
**Trigger:** Turn 150 with no victory path committed.
- Pick the path the accumulated empire is already closest to and commit fully. Linear leaders lose by drifting, not by collapsing.

### Fail state
A comfortable, undirected empire that never converts its advantage into a win condition.

### Caveats
- Comandante Generals are era-bound and retire; banking them without using them wastes the ability.
- Movement advantage shrinks on rough terrain and once rivals have roads and railroads.
- The bonus is broad but shallow. It amplifies a plan, it does not supply one.

### Guide must account for
- General expiry tracked per era, with a use-or-lose prompt at each era transition.
- Settle radius planning that assumes the extra movement point, otherwise the advantage evaporates unused.
- A primary victory path chosen separately, since the ability is path-agnostic.
