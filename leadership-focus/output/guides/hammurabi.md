# Hammurabi — Leadership Focus Guide

**Shape:** Countdown
**Curve:** Spike | **Conversion:** Conditional | **Condition cost:** Base per-turn Science permanently halved
**Convert by:** turn 120

> The ability has an expiry. The guide is a timer wrapped around a conversion deadline. Its most important content is not the build order, it is the turn the plan stops and what replaces it.

## Phase 1 — Arm
*Turns: 1 to roughly 30*

Reach the window with everything already staged. Nothing in this phase is optional; staging done after the window opens is spent from the budget.

- Turn 1: identify the target. Hammurabi's window is short enough that target selection cannot wait for scouting to finish.
- Turns 1-15: scout toward the target only. Exploration away from the target is a luxury this shape cannot afford.
- Turns 10-30: production goes to the units or charges the window will consume. Infrastructure that pays back after 120 is deferred.
- Pre-window checkpoint: confirm Base per-turn Science permanently halved is satisfied and units are within striking distance, not at home.

## Phase 2 — Window
*Turns: roughly 30 to 120*

Convert tempo into permanent assets. Cities, wonders, or improvements. Not kills, not gold, not map presence.


**Checkpoints:**
- *midpoint of window*: Is at least half the objective secured? → if yes: proceed; if no: Reduce scope now. Take one city instead of three. Partial conversion beats total expiry.
- *120*: Has the tempo become something permanent? → if yes: Proceed to Phase 3.; if no: Abort branch.

## Phase 3 — Digest
*Turns: 120 onward*

Stabilize what was taken. This phase is longer than both prior phases combined and most guides for this shape underwrite it badly.

- Amenity and loyalty audit on every acquired city before any new construction.
- Infrastructure catch-up: the arming phase deliberately skipped this, and the bill comes due now.
- Choose a victory path. Hammurabi has no leader-specific direction from here; Actively behind. The penalty outlives the benefit.

## Abort branch
**Trigger:** Window closed with no permanent gain, or three stalled turns before the midpoint.
- Make peace immediately. Prolonging a failed window converts a bad position into a lost game.
- Disband surplus units. The maintenance is now pure loss.
- Switch to the generic development plan for the empire you actually have.

## Fail state
The window closed and nothing permanent was gained. Hammurabi is now an ordinary civ with an army it cannot afford and grievances it cannot repay.

## Caveats
- The science halving is permanent and never improves. Only the benefit expires.
- Later-era Eurekas require conditions that are difficult or slow to satisfy deliberately.
- A tech lead that is not converted into something material is worth nothing once rivals catch up.

## Guide must account for
- A Eureka availability audit per era, so the decline is visible before it bites.
- A hard conversion deadline: the tech lead must become military, wonders, or territory by a set point.
- An explicit late-game plan that assumes a science deficit rather than a lead.
