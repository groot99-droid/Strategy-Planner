# Teddy Roosevelt (Bull Moose) — Strategic Planning Guide

## Civilization VI Profile

**Civilization:** America  |  **Leader Title:** President of the United States
**Source:** New Frontier Pass (Persona Packs)

**Leader Ability — Antiquities and Parks (Bull Moose persona of Roosevelt Corollary)**
The default (non-persona) Teddy Roosevelt's ability, Roosevelt Corollary, grants units +5 Combat Strength on his home continent and +1 Appeal to all tiles in any city containing a National Park. When Persona Packs are enabled, Roosevelt splits into two alternate personas: Rough Rider (keeps a Roosevelt Corollary variant focused on the home-continent combat bonus and doubled Envoys to trade-route city-states) and Bull Moose, whose ability 'Antiquities and Parks' instead grants bonus Science on tiles of Breathtaking Appeal adjacent to a Natural Wonder or Mountain, and bonus Culture on Breathtaking tiles adjacent to a Wonder or Woods — a peaceful, conservation-and-culture playstyle built around National Parks and high-Appeal terrain.

**Agenda — Big Stick Policy**
Roosevelt (in both personas) likes peaceful civilizations that share his continent and dislikes any civilization that starts a war on that continent.

**Civilization Ability — Founding Fathers**
America's civilization ability has evolved across versions: in the base game it halved the time to earn government legacy bonuses; in Rise and Fall it instead converts all Diplomatic policy slots in the current government into Wildcard slots; Gathering Storm added +1 Diplomatic Favor per turn for each Wildcard slot in the current government, making America's policy setup unusually flexible.

**Unique Units**
- Rough Rider (replaces Cuirassier) — An Industrial era heavy cavalry unit with a Combat Strength bonus on Hills and reduced maintenance, reflecting Roosevelt's historical cavalry regiment.
- P-51 Mustang (replaces Fighter) — A Modern era air unit with bonus attack against other fighters, extra range, and faster experience gain, giving America strong air superiority in the late game.

**Unique Infrastructure**
- Film Studio (Building) — Replaces the Broadcast Center and adds extra Tourism pressure toward other civilizations in the Modern era, supporting a Culture/Tourism victory.

### Historical Background
Theodore 'Teddy' Roosevelt (1858-1919) was the 26th U.S. President, a Progressive-era reformer known for trust-busting, conservation of public lands, and an assertive foreign policy summarized by his phrase 'speak softly and carry a big stick' (the source of his in-game agenda name). After leaving the presidency he ran for a third term in 1912 as the candidate of the Progressive 'Bull Moose' Party, the persona referenced in this leader's parenthetical name, campaigning on social reform rather than the more militaristic image of his earlier 'Rough Rider' cavalry service in the Spanish-American War. His dual legacy as both war hero and national-parks conservationist is split directly into Civ VI's two Roosevelt personas.

## Leadership Focus Guide

**Shape:** Linear
**Curve:** Ramp | **Conversion:** Permanent | **Condition cost:** Cannot chop; forfeits the standard production-acceleration lever

> Standard era-block guides work. The ability amplifies a plan rather than supplying one, so the guide's real job is making the player choose a direction the amplifier can act on.

### Ancient / Classical
*Turns: 1 to 80*
- Appeal-preserving settlement. Slow, deliberate.
- Choose the victory path now. A permanent, unconditional bonus applied to no particular plan produces a comfortable game and no win.
- Respect the one constraint: Cannot chop; forfeits the standard production-acceleration lever

### Medieval / Renaissance
*Turns: 80 to 160*
- Preserves and appeal yields compounding.
- Era checkpoint: confirm the bonus is being spent, not banked. This is the characteristic failure of the shape.

### Industrial / Modern
*Turns: 160 to 240*
- Peak tourism and passive science.
- Era checkpoint: is the chosen victory path still reachable? Linear leaders can pivot; the flat curve makes it affordable.

### Atomic / Information
*Turns: 240 onward*
- Strong culture position.
- Close out the chosen condition.

### Abort branch
**Trigger:** Turn 150 with no victory path committed.
- Pick the path the accumulated empire is already closest to and commit fully. Linear leaders lose by drifting, not by collapsing.

### Fail state
A comfortable, undirected empire that never converts its advantage into a win condition.

### Caveats
- No chopping means forfeiting the standard production acceleration lever for the entire game.
- Mines and Industrial Zones destroy the appeal the whole strategy depends on.
- Appeal-based yields need the Preserve civic to fully pay, which gates the payoff.

### Guide must account for
- Appeal-aware district placement rules that override normal adjacency instincts.
- An alternative production plan, since chopping is off the table.
- Appeal as a tracked per-tile value that improvements can silently degrade.
