# Teddy Roosevelt (Bull Moose) — Leadership Focus Guide

**Shape:** Linear
**Curve:** Ramp | **Conversion:** Permanent | **Condition cost:** Cannot chop; forfeits the standard production-acceleration lever

> Standard era-block guides work. The ability amplifies a plan rather than supplying one, so the guide's real job is making the player choose a direction the amplifier can act on.

## Ancient / Classical
*Turns: 1 to 80*
- Appeal-preserving settlement. Slow, deliberate.
- Choose the victory path now. A permanent, unconditional bonus applied to no particular plan produces a comfortable game and no win.
- Respect the one constraint: Cannot chop; forfeits the standard production-acceleration lever

## Medieval / Renaissance
*Turns: 80 to 160*
- Preserves and appeal yields compounding.
- Era checkpoint: confirm the bonus is being spent, not banked. This is the characteristic failure of the shape.

## Industrial / Modern
*Turns: 160 to 240*
- Peak tourism and passive science.
- Era checkpoint: is the chosen victory path still reachable? Linear leaders can pivot; the flat curve makes it affordable.

## Atomic / Information
*Turns: 240 onward*
- Strong culture position.
- Close out the chosen condition.

## Abort branch
**Trigger:** Turn 150 with no victory path committed.
- Pick the path the accumulated empire is already closest to and commit fully. Linear leaders lose by drifting, not by collapsing.

## Fail state
A comfortable, undirected empire that never converts its advantage into a win condition.

## Caveats
- No chopping means forfeiting the standard production acceleration lever for the entire game.
- Mines and Industrial Zones destroy the appeal the whole strategy depends on.
- Appeal-based yields need the Preserve civic to fully pay, which gates the payoff.

## Guide must account for
- Appeal-aware district placement rules that override normal adjacency instincts.
- An alternative production plan, since chopping is off the table.
- Appeal as a tracked per-tile value that improvements can silently degrade.
