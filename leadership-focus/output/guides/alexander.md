# Alexander — Leadership Focus Guide

**Shape:** Monitor
**Curve:** Ramp | **Conversion:** Conditional | **Condition cost:** Must stay at war to keep the engine running

> The core loop is a stat check, not a build order. These guides fail hardest when written as sequences, because the player's real job is watching one number and responding to it.

## Setup
*Turns: 1 to roughly 25*

Establish the thing being monitored and the infrastructure that lets you respond to it.

- Name the watched stat on turn 1: Current war/peace state and next-target readiness
- Establish its bands before it matters, so the player recognizes trouble the first time rather than the third.
- Build the response capacity. A monitor with no available response is just anxiety.

## Standing Loop
*Turns: 25 to end of game*

This replaces the turn list entirely. The guide is this loop plus the era checkpoints.

- Read Current war/peace state and next-target readiness.
- Green band: continue current build. No action.
- Amber band: begin the correction now. Amber is the acting band; red is the failing band.
- Red band: the ability is off or about to be. Everything else is deprioritized until it clears.

## Era Checkpoints
*Turns: each era boundary*

Re-read the role. Monitor leaders change what the stat means across eras.

- Ancient/Classical: Encampment build-out and first wars.
- Medieval/Renaissance: Peak. Continuous war with no domestic penalty.
- Industrial/Modern: Still warring, but tech parity becomes the binding constraint.
- Atomic/Information: Functional, no longer special.

## Abort branch
**Trigger:** Watched stat sits in the red band for more than roughly 15 turns with no recovery path.
- Stop paying the Must stay at war to keep the engine running. If the bonus is unreachable, its price is pure loss.
- Rebuild as a generic civ around whatever the monitored strategy already produced.

## Fail state
The stat crossed into red and stayed. For Alexander, Peace switches the kit off. There is no reward for consolidation phases.

## Caveats
- Peace switches the kit off. There is no reward for consolidation phases.
- No war weariness is not no unhappiness. Loyalty and amenities in captured cities still bite.
- Science from unit training only pays while you keep training. Stopping stops the science.

## Guide must account for
- A rolling target queue with the next war identified before the current one ends.
- Loyalty, not weariness, as the monitored stat during occupation.
- A continuous unit production line treated as a science building, not a military decision.
