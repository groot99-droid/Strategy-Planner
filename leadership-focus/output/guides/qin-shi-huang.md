# Qin Shi Huang — Leadership Focus Guide

**Shape:** Countdown
**Curve:** Spike | **Conversion:** Permanent | **Condition cost:** Builder charges spent on wonders are charges not spent on improvements
**Convert by:** turn 110

> The ability has an expiry. The guide is a timer wrapped around a conversion deadline. Its most important content is not the build order, it is the turn the plan stops and what replaces it.

## Phase 1 — Arm
*Turns: 1 to roughly 30*

Reach the window with everything already staged. Nothing in this phase is optional; staging done after the window opens is spent from the budget.

- Turn 1: identify the target. Qin Shi Huang's window is short enough that target selection cannot wait for scouting to finish.
- Turns 1-15: scout toward the target only. Exploration away from the target is a luxury this shape cannot afford.
- Turns 10-30: production goes to the units or charges the window will consume. Infrastructure that pays back after 110 is deferred.
- Pre-window checkpoint: confirm Builder charges spent on wonders are charges not spent on improvements is satisfied and units are within striking distance, not at home.

## Phase 2 — Window
*Turns: roughly 30 to 110*

Convert tempo into permanent assets. Cities, wonders, or improvements. Not kills, not gold, not map presence.


**Checkpoints:**
- *midpoint of window*: Is at least half the objective secured? → if yes: proceed; if no: Reduce scope now. Take one city instead of three. Partial conversion beats total expiry.
- *110*: Has the tempo become something permanent? → if yes: Proceed to Phase 3.; if no: Abort branch.

## Phase 3 — Digest
*Turns: 110 onward*

Stabilize what was taken. This phase is longer than both prior phases combined and most guides for this shape underwrite it badly.

- Amenity and loyalty audit on every acquired city before any new construction.
- Infrastructure catch-up: the arming phase deliberately skipped this, and the bill comes due now.
- Choose a victory path. Qin Shi Huang has no leader-specific direction from here; Ordinary civ with an unusually good wonder collection.

## Abort branch
**Trigger:** Window closed with no permanent gain, or three stalled turns before the midpoint.
- Make peace immediately. Prolonging a failed window converts a bad position into a lost game.
- Disband surplus units. The maintenance is now pure loss.
- Switch to the generic development plan for the empire you actually have.

## Fail state
The window closed and nothing permanent was gained. Qin Shi Huang is now an ordinary civ with an army it cannot afford and grievances it cannot repay.

## Caveats
- Charges spent on Wonders are charges not spent on tile improvements, so the terrain stays weak.
- A rival completing the wonder first wastes the charges entirely with no refund.
- The ability covers Ancient and Classical wonders only. It has a hard expiry.

## Guide must account for
- A wonder shortlist with pre-planned fallbacks for each one being sniped.
- A Builder charge budget explicitly split between wonders and improvements.
- The era boundary where the ability stops, treated as a scheduled transition.
