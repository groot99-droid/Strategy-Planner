# Jadwiga — Strategic Planning Guide

## Civilization VI Profile

**Civilization:** Poland  |  **Leader Title:** none
**Source:** Base Game

**Leader Ability — Lithuanian Union**
Foreign cities that lose territory to a Polish Culture Bomb convert to Poland's religion, and Jadwiga gains extra Gold, Culture, and Faith from Relics along with stronger Holy Site adjacency bonuses, blending Poland's territorial and religious games.

**Agenda — Saint**
Jadwiga focuses on accumulating Faith and building up religion, and she favors other leaders who invest heavily in Faith generation.

**Civilization Ability — Golden Liberty**
Poland claims all surrounding unowned tiles whenever it builds an Encampment or Fort within its territory, and one of its government's Military policy slots becomes a flexible Wildcard slot, rewarding aggressive early border expansion.

**Unique Units**
- Winged Hussar (replaces Cuirassier) — A powerful heavy cavalry unit that deals bonus damage and ignores Zone of Control when attacking damaged units, making it excellent at breaking through weakened enemy lines.

**Unique Infrastructure**
- Sukiennice (Building) — Replaces the Market and provides additional Gold and Great Merchant points, strengthening Poland's commercial output.

### Historical Background
Jadwiga (reigned 1384-1399) was crowned 'King' (rather than Queen, to underscore her full sovereign authority) of Poland at age ten, and her 1386 marriage to Grand Duke Jogaila of Lithuania created the Polish-Lithuanian union that grew into one of early modern Europe's largest states. A devoted patron of the Church, she funded the restoration of Kraków's university and pushed for the Christianization of pagan Lithuania. Her role in forging a lasting personal union between two realms is echoed in the leader ability's name, Lithuanian Union, while her religious patronage is reflected in her Faith-focused agenda and Relic bonuses.

## Leadership Focus Guide

**Shape:** Gate
**Curve:** Ramp | **Conversion:** Permanent | **Condition cost:** Guaranteed diplomatic hostility from every neighbor

> The strategy does not begin on a turn. It begins on an unlock. This needs two complete guides with a trigger between them, not one guide with a note in the middle.

### Pre-Gate
*Turns: 1 until the gate opens*

Survive, develop, and stage. This is a real phase with its own complete build order, not a waiting room.

- State the gate explicitly on turn 1 so the player knows what they are working toward: Religion founded
- Give an estimated arrival turn AND the research or civic path that gets there fastest.
- Build the prerequisites the gate assumes but does not provide. Guaranteed diplomatic hostility from every neighbor
- Maintain a defensive floor. The gate does not protect you while you wait for it.

### Gate Check
*Turns: variable*

A single decision point, not a phase.


### Post-Gate
*Turns: gate onward*

The actual leader strategy, now live.

- Peak. Forced conversion and territorial pressure.
- Run the closing-window check: most Gate abilities are also time-limited on the far end. Identify when parity arrives.
- Convert before parity. Permanent

### Abort branch
**Trigger:** Gate opens with staging incomplete and a rival is already at parity.
- Do not open the strategy. Playing an unstaged Gate leader into a prepared opponent is the worst outcome available.
- Fold into a generic plan built on whatever the pre-gate phase produced.

### Fail state
The gate opened, the window closed, and the strategy never ran. The pre-gate phase was treated as filler and produced nothing usable.

### Caveats
- The ability requires a founded religion first, which gates the whole strategy.
- Guaranteed grievances from every affected neighbor, with no diplomatic recovery.
- Border Encampments are exposed positions by definition.

### Guide must account for
- Religion founding as a hard prerequisite gate before any Encampment placement.
- A defensive plan for exposed forward Encampments.
- Acceptance of permanent diplomatic isolation as a stated cost of the plan.
