# Jayavarman VII — Leadership Focus Guide

**Shape:** Linear
**Curve:** Flat | **Conversion:** Permanent | **Condition cost:** River dependency

> Standard era-block guides work. The ability amplifies a plan rather than supplying one, so the guide's real job is making the player choose a direction the amplifier can act on.

## Ancient / Classical
*Turns: 1 to 80*
- River Holy Sites providing Food, Housing, and Faith at once.
- Choose the victory path now. A permanent, unconditional bonus applied to no particular plan produces a comfortable game and no win.
- Respect the one constraint: River dependency

## Medieval / Renaissance
*Turns: 80 to 160*
- Population engine compounding.
- Era checkpoint: confirm the bonus is being spent, not banked. This is the characteristic failure of the shape.

## Industrial / Modern
*Turns: 160 to 240*
- Large high-population empire.
- Era checkpoint: is the chosen victory path still reachable? Linear leaders can pivot; the flat curve makes it affordable.

## Atomic / Information
*Turns: 240 onward*
- Flexible; population supports any path.
- Close out the chosen condition.

## Abort branch
**Trigger:** Turn 150 with no victory path committed.
- Pick the path the accumulated empire is already closest to and commit fully. Linear leaders lose by drifting, not by collapsing.

## Fail state
A comfortable, undirected empire that never converts its advantage into a win condition.

## Caveats
- River dependency constrains settling more than it appears, especially for later cities.
- Flooding threatens exactly the tiles the strategy is built on.
- Holy Site placement for the river bonus can conflict with optimal adjacency for other districts.

## Guide must account for
- River-first settle planning across the whole intended empire, not just the capital.
- Aqueduct and dam sequencing relative to Holy Site placement.
- A victory path chosen separately, since population is an input rather than a direction.
