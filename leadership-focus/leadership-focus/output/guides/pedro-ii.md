# Pedro II — Leadership Focus Guide

**Shape:** Monitor
**Curve:** Ramp | **Conversion:** Permanent | **Condition cost:** Rainforest dependency; must not clear it

> The core loop is a stat check, not a build order. These guides fail hardest when written as sequences, because the player's real job is watching one number and responding to it.

## Setup
*Turns: 1 to roughly 25*

Establish the thing being monitored and the infrastructure that lets you respond to it.

- Name the watched stat on turn 1: Own Great Person points vs. leading rival's points, per category
- Establish its bands before it matters, so the player recognizes trouble the first time rather than the third.
- Build the response capacity. A monitor with no available response is just anxiety.

## Standing Loop
*Turns: 25 to end of game*

This replaces the turn list entirely. The guide is this loop plus the era checkpoints.

- Read Own Great Person points vs. leading rival's points, per category.
- Green band: continue current build. No action.
- Amber band: begin the correction now. Amber is the acting band; red is the failing band.
- Red band: the ability is off or about to be. Everything else is deprioritized until it clears.

## Era Checkpoints
*Turns: each era boundary*

Re-read the role. Monitor leaders change what the stat means across eras.

- Ancient/Classical: Rainforest-adjacent district placement.
- Medieval/Renaissance: Great Person accumulation accelerating.
- Industrial/Modern: Great Person monopoly across chosen categories.
- Atomic/Information: Strong in whichever category was monopolized.

## Abort branch
**Trigger:** Watched stat sits in the red band for more than roughly 15 turns with no recovery path.
- Stop paying the Rainforest dependency; must not clear it. If the bonus is unreachable, its price is pure loss.
- Rebuild as a generic civ around whatever the monitored strategy already produced.

## Fail state
The stat crossed into red and stayed. For Pedro II, Rainforest suppresses production and is constantly tempting to clear, which breaks the engine.

## Caveats
- Rainforest suppresses production and is constantly tempting to clear, which breaks the engine.
- Great People are contested. A lost race is points spent for nothing.
- The refund only applies on recruitment, so patronage spending does not compound the same way.

## Guide must account for
- A Great Person priority list committed to early, since spreading thin loses every race.
- Rival Great Person point totals as a monitored value.
- A production plan that accepts rainforest penalties rather than clearing them.
