# Tokugawa — Leadership Focus Guide

**Shape:** Linear
**Curve:** Flat | **Conversion:** Permanent | **Condition cost:** International trade heavily penalized; forfeits foreign trade income entirely

> Standard era-block guides work. The ability amplifies a plan rather than supplying one, so the guide's real job is making the player choose a direction the amplifier can act on.

## Ancient / Classical
*Turns: 1 to 80*
- Establishing the capital cluster and first internal routes.
- Choose the victory path now. A permanent, unconditional bonus applied to no particular plan produces a comfortable game and no win.
- Respect the one constraint: International trade heavily penalized; forfeits foreign trade income entirely

## Medieval / Renaissance
*Turns: 80 to 160*
- Domestic route yields stacking.
- Era checkpoint: confirm the bonus is being spent, not banked. This is the characteristic failure of the shape.

## Industrial / Modern
*Turns: 160 to 240*
- Strong self-contained economy.
- Era checkpoint: is the chosen victory path still reachable? Linear leaders can pivot; the flat curve makes it affordable.

## Atomic / Information
*Turns: 240 onward*
- Solid, insulated.
- Close out the chosen condition.

## Abort branch
**Trigger:** Turn 150 with no victory path committed.
- Pick the path the accumulated empire is already closest to and commit fully. Linear leaders lose by drifting, not by collapsing.

## Fail state
A comfortable, undirected empire that never converts its advantage into a win condition.

## Caveats
- International trade income is forfeited entirely, not reduced. That is a whole mechanic given away.
- Domestic routes need many cities to be worth much, so the kit rewards width you must build first.
- Isolation reduces diplomatic leverage and information about rival progress.

## Guide must account for
- City count treated as the multiplier on the entire trade strategy.
- An explicit prohibition on international routes, since they will look tempting.
- An information plan, because isolation means flying blind on rival progress.
