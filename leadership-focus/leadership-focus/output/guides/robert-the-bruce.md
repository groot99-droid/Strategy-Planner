# Robert the Bruce — Leadership Focus Guide

**Shape:** Monitor
**Curve:** Ramp | **Conversion:** Conditional | **Condition cost:** Permanent Ecstatic amenity upkeep across the whole empire

> The core loop is a stat check, not a build order. These guides fail hardest when written as sequences, because the player's real job is watching one number and responding to it.

## Setup
*Turns: 1 to roughly 25*

Establish the thing being monitored and the infrastructure that lets you respond to it.

- Name the watched stat on turn 1: Empire-wide amenity level (city-by-city)
- Establish its bands before it matters, so the player recognizes trouble the first time rather than the third.
- Build the response capacity. A monitor with no available response is just anxiety.

## Standing Loop
*Turns: 25 to end of game*

This replaces the turn list entirely. The guide is this loop plus the era checkpoints.

- Read Empire-wide amenity level (city-by-city).
- Green band: continue current build. No action.
- Amber band: begin the correction now. Amber is the acting band; red is the failing band.
- Red band: the ability is off or about to be. Everything else is deprioritized until it clears.

## Era Checkpoints
*Turns: each era boundary*

Re-read the role. Monitor leaders change what the stat means across eras.

- Ancient/Classical: Amenity infrastructure build-up before any bonus exists.
- Medieval/Renaissance: Bonuses live, conditional on maintaining Ecstatic.
- Industrial/Modern: Strong while the amenity state holds.
- Atomic/Information: Same, still conditional.

## Abort branch
**Trigger:** Watched stat sits in the red band for more than roughly 15 turns with no recovery path.
- Stop paying the Permanent Ecstatic amenity upkeep across the whole empire. If the bonus is unreachable, its price is pure loss.
- Rebuild as a generic civ around whatever the monitored strategy already produced.

## Fail state
The stat crossed into red and stayed. For Robert the Bruce, An amenity crash removes the bonus empire-wide instantly, not gradually.

## Caveats
- An amenity crash removes the bonus empire-wide instantly, not gradually.
- Luxuries acquired by trade expire, so the amenity state has a renewal schedule you must track.
- Wide play makes Ecstatic across all cities practically unachievable.

## Guide must account for
- Amenities as a per-turn monitored stat with a warning threshold, not a build item.
- A city count cap derived from available luxuries rather than available land.
- A luxury renewal calendar tied to trade deal expiry dates.
