# Cleopatra (Ptolemaic) — Leadership Focus Guide

**Shape:** Linear
**Curve:** Ramp | **Conversion:** Permanent | **Condition cost:** River and floodplain dependency

> Standard era-block guides work. The ability amplifies a plan rather than supplying one, so the guide's real job is making the player choose a direction the amplifier can act on.

## Ancient / Classical
*Turns: 1 to 80*
- River and floodplain settling with early Food and Culture.
- Choose the victory path now. A permanent, unconditional bonus applied to no particular plan produces a comfortable game and no win.
- Respect the one constraint: River and floodplain dependency

## Medieval / Renaissance
*Turns: 80 to 160*
- Appeal advantage compounding into culture.
- Era checkpoint: confirm the bonus is being spent, not banked. This is the characteristic failure of the shape.

## Industrial / Modern
*Turns: 160 to 240*
- National Parks and Seaside Resorts on inverted-appeal terrain.
- Era checkpoint: is the chosen victory path still reachable? Linear leaders can pivot; the flat curve makes it affordable.

## Atomic / Information
*Turns: 240 onward*
- Strong tourism position.
- Close out the chosen condition.

## Abort branch
**Trigger:** Turn 150 with no victory path committed.
- Pick the path the accumulated empire is already closest to and commit fully. Linear leaders lose by drifting, not by collapsing.

## Fail state
A comfortable, undirected empire that never converts its advantage into a win condition.

## Caveats
- Floodplains still flood. The appeal inversion does not prevent the damage.
- Dam construction competes for production exactly when growth is most valuable.
- River dependency constrains settling more tightly than it first appears.

## Guide must account for
- A flood risk versus appeal tradeoff decision per city, with dam timing attached.
- The two-phase nature of the payoff, since early Food and late Parks need different plans.
- River availability assessed at settle time across the whole intended empire.
